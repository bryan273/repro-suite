Main task: Paper Explainer (about 10 minutes)主要任务：Paper Explainer（约 10 分钟）

Turn a paper into a video把一篇论文变成视频

paper-explainer-aiid.vercel.app

  1. Open the site and press Make a video. Paste an arXiv link, a PDF link, or a DOI of a paper you know (or use arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).
  2. Choose the language and voice you like, then start. Watch the progress bar.
  3. While you wait, scroll the page: look at Examples, How it works and Pricing.
  4. When the video is ready, watch at least the first 30 seconds. Is the voice clear? Do the on-screen quotes look right?
  5. Try the 中文 button at the top right. Is anything missing or odd?
  1. 打开网站，点击 Make a video。粘贴一篇你熟悉的论文的 arXiv 链接、PDF 链接或 DOI（也可以用 arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762）。
  2. 选择你喜欢的语言和声音，然后开始，留意进度条。
  3. 等待期间滚动页面，看看 Examples、How it works 和 Pricing。
  4. 视频完成后，至少看前 30 秒。声音清楚吗？屏幕上的引文看起来对吗？
  5. 点右上角的“中文”按钮，有没有缺失或奇怪的地方？

Other apps (pick one, about 5 minutes)其他应用（任选一个，约 5 分钟）

Paper Repro Agent

paper-repro-aiid.vercel.app/repro

An agent re-runs a paper's results from scratch and gives a verdict. Open a recorded run (instant, free), read the verdict, and tell us if you understand what it says.

智能体会从头重新运行论文的结果并给出结论。打开一个已录制的运行（即时、免费），读一下结论，告诉我们你是否理解。

Repro Lite

repro-lite-aiid.vercel.app

Type a claim such as "Isolation Forest reaches ROC AUC about 0.95 on breast cancer" and press Run check. It runs in your browser and takes 1 to 2 minutes.

输入一个结论，例如“Isolation Forest 在 breast cancer 数据集上 ROC AUC 约 0.95”，然后点击 Run check。它在你的浏览器里运行，需要 1 到 2 分钟。

Reading Companion

reading-companion-aiid.vercel.app

Ask a question about a paper (for example "What is the main result of arXiv 1607.01759?") and press Start reading. An answer with numbered sources appears in about 2 to 3 minutes.

就一篇论文提问（例如“arXiv 1607.01759 的主要结论是什么？”），然后点击 Start reading。大约 2 到 3 分钟后会出现带编号来源的回答。

If something goes wrong如果出了问题

  • A video stuck for more than 8 minutes: tell the team, and try a different paper link.
  • An answer is slow: the AI service can be slow at busy times. Wait a minute or try again.
  • Orange dot in the Paper Repro Agent top bar: live runs are offline, so use the recorded samples.
  • 视频卡住超过 8 分钟：告诉团队，并换一个论文链接再试。
  • 回答很慢：繁忙时 AI 服务可能较慢，请等一分钟或重试。
  • Paper Repro Agent 顶栏出现橙色圆点：在线运行暂时不可用，请使用已录制的示例。
